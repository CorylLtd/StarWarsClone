# Star Wars (Atari 1983) – HUD / cockpit / font / Death-Star miniature, decoded from source

Companion to `shapes-hud.json` (generated by `decode_hud.py` + `assemble_hud.py` from the source tree).
Conventions as in `shapes-dogfight.md`: VG screen units, +X right, +Y up, origin = screen centre, default
SCALE word `7200`; visible area ≈ X ±510, Y −612..600; one Y unit is physically ≈ ⅔ of an X unit (the 2-D
`ASPECT` macro stretches Y by 3/2 where a shape must look round). Hex unless a trailing dot.

## 1. Cockpit / gun-frame pictures (`WSVROM.MAC VGSTTR, VGSTBR, VGSTBM, VGSTTL, VGSTBL`)

* Five stamps in the real VG ROM, `M.=10`, **no ASPECT** (the `ENDASPECT` at line 1410 is in force), so
  the JSON strokes are plain VG units.
* Each stamp first does an absolute blank move to a screen corner and then draws with `CXY`-relative
  `AON/AOFF`, so the stored coordinates are absolute (for zero yoke shift):

| Stamp | Corner move | Local→screen | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| STTR | `VOFF VGLIMR,VGOFFY` = (480,−104), `CXY −10,0` | (580+10x, −104+10y) | top-right side gun |
| STBR | (480,−552), `CXY −10,3` | (580+10x, −582+10y) | bottom-right gun |
| STBM | (0,−552), `CXY 0,7` | (10x, −622+10y) | nose/hood, bottom centre (partly below the visible edge) |
| STTL | (−480,−104), `CXY 10,0` | (−580+10x, −104+10y) | top-left gun (mirror) |
| STBL | (−480,−552), `CXY 10,3` | (−580+10x, −582+10y) | bottom-left gun (mirror) |

* Colours are inside the stamps: **BLU 80** for the gun shaft and gun tip, **RED 80** for the collar and the
  dish (wing panel); STBM: BLU hood outline, RED inner detail. All intensity 7. Each ends with `CNTR`.
* Yoke shift (`WSSITE.MAC VWPLAN`): before every stamp a blank long vector (dy=`LZ.GY`, dx=`LZ.GX`) is
  emitted from the centre, so the whole frame slides. `LZ.GX = sign·(|SI.RSX|·110)>>8` (±54 max),
  `LZ.GY = sign·(|SI.RSY|·80)>>8` (±39 max) from the ±127 yaw/pitch control values. Draw order:
  STBM, STTR, STBL, STTL, STBR (then `LEAY 30.(Y)`). The blue gun tips are exactly where the laser
  bolts start: STTR tip ends at (390,−104) = `VGLIMR−90, VGOFFY`; STBR tip at ≈(395,−517) = `VGLIMR−85,
  VGLIMB+35`; and the bolts get the same `LZ.GX/LZ.GY` shift.

## 2. Shield gauge (`WSGAS.MAC VWGAS`, pictures in `WSVROM.MAC` "GAS GAUGE")

Drawn every dogfight frame (`VEWS0` → `VWGAS`), top centre, in this VG sequence:
1. `COLOR TGCLR[S.GAS]` – **1–2 RED, 3–4 YELLOW, 5–9 GREEN** (lum 80); lum forced to FF for 20 frames after a
   refuel (`S.CAN`). S.GAS starts at 6 + (option nibble & 3) = 6..9, so a fresh game is green; the gauge
   turns yellow at 4 and red at 2. At 0 (and no hit animation running) the gauge is replaced by the
   "SHIELD GONE" message (2× scale, cycling colour, ≈528 wide, centred at y≈460..508).
2. `JSRL VJFUEL`: the word **SHIELD** with the first character's lower-left at (−72, 456) (row VGRW4),
   144 wide, 24 tall, in the gauge colour.
3. Blank long vector to the base point (0, 552) (`VGRW0`), then `JSRL VJGA<S.GAS>` (whole gauge) – or
   the hit-animation variants (below).
4. Blank long vector (−12, −48) → pen at (−12, 504); one digit (`VWBYT1`, 16×24 cell) = shield count,
   colour = gauge colour (or flash colour while animating). `VGCNTR`.

Gauge geometry (`M.=10`, `ASPECT`: 1 horizontal unit = 30 VG, 1 vertical unit = 15 VG). For n units, each
half (right = +x, left mirrored) is a bright diagonal from the base point out/down to (±30n, −15n), a vertical
up to (±30n, 0), then the base line back to the centre in 30-unit steps with a downward tick at every step
k = 1..n−1 of height 15·h(k), h = 3/6/9 at k = 3/6/9, else 1 – a "butterfly"/winged bracket that gets wider
and deeper with more shields (n=6: ±180 wide, 90 deep). The JSON has every `VJGA0..9` and `VJGU0..9` as
stroke lists relative to (0,552); `VJG<n>` / `VJG<n>A` (base line only, with/without the outer tick) are
the animation frames and can be generated with `gauge(n,'G'|'GA_')` in `decode_hud.py`.

Hit animation (`GSVNEW`, after `DO1GAS` has decremented S.GAS): the old gauge is drawn in the **flash colour**
(`VJFLS`, colours 1..7 at lum FF cycling every frame) for 10−S.GAS frames; then the new gauge is drawn in
its colour with a flashing overlay of the old upstroke for a frame, then a flashing base-line overlay that
shrinks by one step per frame (`TGDRW` from index 2·S.GAS down to 0); then normal.

## 3. Score / wave text (`WSVROM.MAC VGTITL`, `WSMAIN.MAC VWMES`)

| Item | Colour | Lower-left of first char | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| "SCORE" | RED 80 | (−408, 528) = (VGLIML+3·24, VGRW1) | 5 chars |
| "WAVE" | RED 80 | (384, 528) = (VGLIMR−4·24, VGRW1) | 4 chars |
| score digits | GREEN 80 | (−480, 480) = (VGLIML, VGRW3) | 8 BCD digits, 6 leading zeros suppressed (blanks still advance 24), commas after digit 7 and 4 once a digit has printed |
| last score added | YELLOW, lum = SC.ADT/2 (SC.ADT: FF → −8/frame) | (−432, 432) = (VGLIML+2·24, VGRW5) | 6 digits + commas, 5 zeros suppressed; visible while SC.ADT ≠ 0 |
| wave number | GREEN 80 | (312, 528) = (VGLIMR−7·24, VGRW1) | 2 BCD digits, 1 zero suppressed |
| first-wave hints | WHT / RED | (−176,384) / (−212,384) (VGRW7) | "SHOOT FIREBALLS" / "SHOOT TIE FIGHTERS" alternate every 16 frames for the first 100 frames of wave 1 |

Rows: `VGRW0..7` = 552, 528, 504, 480, 456, 432, 408, 384 (24 apart); the 3-D window's top limit is 408.

## 4. Vector font (`WSVGAN.MAC`, Ed Logg's VGAN, `.RADIX 10`, `.BRITE=7`)

* Every glyph is a VG subroutine of `VCTR dx,dy,z` strokes starting at the glyph's lower-left; the pen
  ends **exactly (24, 0)** further right for every glyph (verified by the decoder), so strings are simple
  JSRL sequences (`ALPHA <TEXT>`). Glyph bodies are 16 wide × 24 tall in a 24-unit cell (`VG$C = VG$CW = 24`);
  vertical strings use `VG$CV = 36` spacing (`ALPHAV`). Digits `0` and `5` are the same routines as `O` and `S`.
* The JSON `font.glyphs` maps `" "`, `0–9`, `A–Z`, `'`, `,`, `.`, `:`, `_`, `1/2`, `(c)`, `-` to polylines
  (lists of [x,y]) in glyph-local units, all intensity 7; the glyph routines share tails via `JMPL`
  (resolved by the decoder). `VJNUMS` (the comma between digit groups) is a separate ROM stamp: a tick
  from (−4,−6) to (−2,4) relative to the pen with a net advance of only (+4, 0).
* Placement (`TCMES.MAC DOMESS/MOUT/MESOUT`): COLOR word, blank long vector (dy, dx) from the centre to
  the lower-left of the first character (the `.MESS x,y,colour,<text>` table stores exactly that), one
  JSRL per character, then `VGSCAL`, `VGCNTR`. Text is scaled only by a VG SCALE word emitted before the
  message (e.g. `VGSCAL−100` = 2× for SHIELD GONE / GAME OVER; `VWMOVR` grows GAME OVER from linear scale
  C0 to 00). MESOUT maps ASCII A–Z, 0–9, space, `'`, `,`, `.`, `:` and `@` (→ ©).

## 5. Death-Star miniature (`WSVROM.MAC BMIN / VJ BMIN`, `WSMAIN.MAC VWDTHA`)

* Stamp: `M.=1`, `ASPECT` (stored Y already ×3/2). WHITE trench line (intensity 6) from (49,15) to (−49,−15);
  GREEN 16-segment circle of radius 50 with per-segment intensity 5 (dark, lower-left) / 6 / 7 (lit, right);
  RED 8-segment dish outline around (22,40) plus three RED inner lines; GREEN "farmland" polyline and one
  extra GREEN line lower-right. `VJBMN2` enters after the WHITE colour word (caller picks the trench colour).
* Dogfight rule (`VWDTHA`, called from `VEWS0` and `VEWHPA`): the Death Star is a point at infinity along
  world +X, so its view-space direction is simply the player's unit-vector components (AX, AY, AZ). Shown iff
  `AX > 0`, `|AY| < AX`, `|AZ| < AX`. Position = `OBJCEN` with DVD=AX, YP=AY, ZP=AZ → screen
  (512·AY/AX, 512·AZ/AX + VGOFFY). Drawn with `SCALE 7300` (half of the default) → on-screen radius 25 × 37.5,
  then `SCALE 7200`. **It does not grow during the dogfight.**
* Growth happens in the next phase (hyperspace approach, `PHES1G`/`VEWHPB` → `VWDTHB`): the detailed set
  `VJBCIR` (green circle), `VJBTRN` (white trench), `VJBDSH`/`VJBNSD` (red dish + spokes), `VJBFRM` (green
  farmland) and yellow city-light stamps are drawn at `SCALE DT.SCL`, which starts at 7780 and shrinks
  (= grows on screen) by `DT.STP` per frame (DT.STP starts 100 and increases by 60 per frame; the linear byte
  is kept in 0..7F and borrows into the binary nibble) until `DT.SCL ≤ 7310`, while the stars dim by 2/frame.
  The detailed pictures (`M.=32`, circle radius 1600 raw units) are in the JSON under `detailedPictures`;
  the city-light stamps `VJUB1..4/VJLB1..4` were not extracted. The select screen draws `VJBMIN` at scale
  7200 at fixed positions (`TDTH` table); `VEWDX2` draws it at (0, VGOFFY) at scale 7200.

## 6. Windshield flash (`WSGLOW.MAC VWGLW`, `WSVROM.MAC VGFCWN`) and flash colours

* `VWGLW`: if `(S.GLW & 3) != 0` emit `SCALE 7100` (2×), `COLOR WHT FF`, `JSRL VJFCWN`, `SCALE 7200`.
  `VJFCWN` = `VOFF (480,552)`, `VON` to (480,−552), (−480,−552), (−480,552), then `VON 960,552` → (480,1104)
  – at 2× scale all of this lies outside the visible area (±960 / ±1104), and the last stroke is a diagonal
  (surely a typo for a horizontal close). On the arcade monitor the effect is a brief whole-screen white
  flash; implement it as such.
* Trigger: `BG1GLW` sets `S.GLW = 10` (hex) when a fireball hits the windshield (`GNAHIT`), one hit at a time
  (`GS.GLW` gate); `DO1GLW` decrements it each frame (capped at 8 in the trench) → 16 frames of flashing,
  3 on / 1 off. The same event stamps the fireball white and shrinking at its impact point (`GLOWVW`), rolls
  the ship ±20 (hex) and costs one shield unit.
* `VJINIT` (first thing every frame): `CNTR`, `SCAL`, `COLOR BLU`, four zero-length dots at (±480, ±552),
  `CNTR` – faint blue corner marks of the play area.
* Flash colours: `VJFLS` → VG-RAM word `VRFLS`: `COLOR c,FF` with c cycling 1..7 one step per VG frame
  (`WSINT`); `VJMFL` → `VRMFL`: same cycle at lum 80 ("medium flash", used for messages).

## 7. Open questions

1. `SI.RSX/SI.RSY` are documented here as the ±127 yaw/pitch control values feeding the frame shift; their
   producer (in `WSINT.MAC` `RHCTRL`, not fully traced) may apply filtering, so the ±54/±39 maxima are upper bounds.
2. The `VJFCWN` last stroke and its off-screen geometry (see §6) – treat as a full-screen flash.
3. Glyph strokes were decoded with the assembler's `.RADIX 10`; the `VCTR` short-vector encoding is lossless,
   so coordinates are exact. Blank moves inside glyphs are not kept (only visible polylines).
4. City-light stamps (`VJUB*/VJLB*`) and the `TDTH` select-screen positions were not extracted.
5. Colour words in the cockpit stamps use default lum 80; MAME's rendering may look brighter because it
   scales by intensity 7.
